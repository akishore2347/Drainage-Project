How DrainSight AI Operates

A modular computer-vision pipeline built to work without physical sensor hardware.

1
Input & Preprocessing

Accepts high-resolution images and videos. Applies bilateral denoising, CLAHE lighting enhancement, and 640x640 normalization.

OpenCV 4.8
2
YOLOv8 Detection

Identifies drain apertures alongside 4 critical waste classes: plastic bags, leaves, paper debris, and mud sediment.

Ultralytics YOLOv8
3
IoU Overlap Logic

Calculates geometric intersection between waste masks and the drain rim to output an exact percentage blockage metric.

Mathematical Vision
4
Alerting & Reports

Categorizes risk (Clear, Partial, Heavy), issues urgent municipal warning alerts, logs SQLite records, and exports CSV reports.
