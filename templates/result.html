Blockage Estimation Logic
Blockage (%) = (Area(Debris ∩ Drain Opening) / Area(Drain Opening)) × 100

The algorithm uses exact binary masks to calculate the non-overlapping intersection between waste bounding boxes and the primary drain aperture. Overlapping debris is merged to prevent double-counting.

Threshold Standards Applied:
0% – 20%: Normal water flow condition Clear
21% – 60%: Routine maintenance recommended Partially Blocked
> 60%: Critical flood hazard; dispatch cleaning crew Heavily Blocked
