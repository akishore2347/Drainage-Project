How DrainSight AI Detects Blockages

An end-to-end sensorless architecture that transforms standard digital imagery into quantitative drainage obstruction metrics and early warning signals.

High-Level Architecture Diagram
1
1. Media Acquisition

Users upload digital images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) or video clips (MP4, AVI) captured using standard smartphones, handheld cameras, or municipality CCTV streams. No underwater sensors or ultrasonic meters are required.

Input Module
2
2. OpenCV Image Enhancement

Input media undergoes noise reduction via Bilateral Filtering to smooth textures while retaining sharp iron grate edges. CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) boosts contrast in dark culvert shadows.

OpenCV 4.8
3
3. YOLOv8 Object Detection

A trained Ultralytics YOLOv8 convolutional neural network identifies the primary drain_opening and scans for four distinct waste classes: plastic_waste, leaves, paper_waste, and mud_sediment.

Ultralytics YOLOv8
4
4. Geometric IoU Overlap

Instead of naive counts, the system computes the exact pixel intersection between waste bounding boxes and the drain aperture. Overlapping debris masks are unified to prevent double-counting.

Spatial Math
5
5. Threshold Risk Stratification

The calculated obstruction percentage is classified into three standardized risk levels: 0–20% (Clear), 21–60% (Partially Blocked), and >60% (Heavily Blocked).

Risk Engine
6
6. Telemetry & Alerting

The annotated media with bounding boxes and HUD banners is rendered and saved. Results are archived in an SQLite database and rendered on an analytics dashboard with instant CSV report generation.

Blockage Calculation Formulation

Let $\Omega_{\text{drain}} \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ denote the bounding polygon of the primary drainage aperture, and let $\Omega_{\text{waste}} = \bigcup_{i=1}^N \Omega_i$ represent the union of all $N$ detected waste objects.

$\text{Blockage Ratio} = \frac{\text{Area}(\Omega_{\text{drain}} \cap \Omega_{\text{waste}})}{\text{Area}(\Omega_{\text{drain}})} \times 100\%$

This formulation guarantees that debris lying outside the drainage rim does not falsely penalize the blockage score, while clustered trash is accounted for accurately without exceeding 100%.

Optional Watershed Segmentation

In addition to bounding box detection, the system includes an OpenCV Watershed Segmentation algorithm to segment irregular sludge and plastic boundaries from surrounding water and pavement.

  • Otsu Thresholding: Isolates high-contrast foreground debris.
  • Morphological Opening: Eliminates isolated noise pixels.
  • Distance Transform ($L_2$): Identifies sure foreground centers.
  • Topological Watershed: Floyd-Warshall style immersion flood to trace organic debris perimeters.

Why Computer Vision Over Physical Sensors?
Zero Sensor Cost

No expensive submerged flowmeters, pressure sensors, or IoT battery replacements.

Immune to Corrosion

Physical sensors foul and corrode rapidly in acidic sewer wastewater; cameras remain remote.

Mobile Friendly

Municipal workers can snap photos from a smartphone or mount cameras to street sweepers.

Instant Diagnosis

Inference completes in milliseconds on standard laptop CPUs and mobile processors.
