About DrainSight AI

Intelligent Drainage Monitoring and Early Flood Prevention System developed as a computer vision final-year engineering initiative.

Project Objective

DrainSight AI addresses urban water stagnation and flash flooding by automating the visual inspection of storm drains, culverts, and roadside gutters.

By combining OpenCV computer vision preprocessing with Ultralytics YOLOv8 deep learning, the system identifies surface waste (plastic bottles, wrappers, fallen leaves, paper waste, and mud accumulation) and estimates the percentage blockage without requiring physical sensor hardware.

Automated Inspection Flood Mitigation Zero Physical Sensors
Problem Statement

Urban stormwater drainage networks routinely get choked by solid waste, leading to localized inundation, road damage, mosquito breeding, and severe property destruction during monsoon rains.

Traditional municipal drainage monitoring relies on hazardous, labor-intensive manual inspections or fragile submerged ultrasonic sensors that foul within weeks. DrainSight AI introduces an accessible, non-contact, computer-vision alternative deployable on laptops, mobile phones, or patrol vehicles.

Key Benefit: Provides municipal authorities with rapid, quantitative evidence to dispatch cleaning crews before minor blockages escalate into catastrophic road flooding.

Four Functional System Modules
Module 1: Preprocessing

Validates image/video formats, extracts frames, performs Bilateral noise reduction, and applies CLAHE adaptive histogram enhancement for low-light images.

Module 2: AI Detection

Executes YOLOv8 to locate drain aperture coordinates and detect visible debris (plastic, leaves, paper, mud) with confidence metrics.

Module 3: Assessment

Calculates geometric IoU overlap between waste masks and drain opening. Classifies into Clear (0-20%), Partial (21-60%), and Heavy (>60%).

Module 4: Telemetry

Renders annotated output media with HUD banners, logs SQLite records, powers interactive Chart.js charts, and exports CSV reports.

Current System Limitations
  • Visual-Only 2D Surface Detection: Detects surface blockages at the drain grate; cannot see deep subterranean pipe collapses.
  • Extreme Turbidity: Heavily murky floodwater may obscure submerged trash under the surface.
  • Severe Weather Occlusion: Heavy torrential rain splashing directly onto camera lenses can cause optical distortion.
  • Free Hosting Memory Constraints: Video uploads on free cloud tiers (Render) are limited to short clips to avoid out-of-memory errors.
Future Roadmap & Extensions
  • Native Android Application: Offline on-device YOLOv8-TFLite inspection app for field municipal sanitation workers.
  • Autonomous Drone Patrols: Low-altitude aerial inspection of municipal canal banks and storm drains.
  • Municipal GIS & SMS Dispatch: Integration with Google Maps API and SMS alert gateways to automatically notify district engineers.
  • Edge CCTV Cameras: Real-time 24/7 RTSP video stream analysis installed directly over high-risk urban drainage culverts.
